# Receiving payouts in Trust Wallet

CoinPayments holds paid invoices in your CoinPayments balance. To receive them in Trust Wallet:

1. In Trust Wallet, open the coin you accept (for example USDT or USDC), choose Receive, and copy the address for the network you want (Polygon and BSC have low fees).
2. In the CoinPayments dashboard, open wallet or payout settings and add that address as your withdrawal address for each coin. Turn on automatic payouts if the dashboard offers it, otherwise withdraw by hand.
3. Send a small test amount first and confirm it arrives.
4. Keep your Trust Wallet recovery phrase offline. Never put it, or any wallet key, in Ledgerline's settings or env file. Ledgerline never needs it.

Check the current CoinPayments menu names and fees in their dashboard, as they change.
